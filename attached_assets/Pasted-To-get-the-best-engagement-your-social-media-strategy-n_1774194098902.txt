To get the best engagement, your social media strategy needs to balance the algorithm's hunger for consistency with your audience's attention span. 

Here is a comprehensive guide covering the optimal posting rules for each major platform, followed by a 14-day (Week 1 & Week 2) content blueprint tailored for restaurants, home services, retail, professional services, and general business.

### 1. Platform Optimization Cheat Sheet

To maximize reach, keep your captions punchy and stick to a consistent posting rhythm. 

| Platform | Optimal Frequency | Ideal Character Count | Strategy Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instagram** | 3–5 In-feed/Reels weekly, 2+ Stories daily | 138–150 chars (keeps text above the "more" button) | Visual storytelling, high-quality video, behind-the-scenes. |
| **Facebook** | 1–2 times per day | 40–80 chars (short text gets the highest engagement) | Community building, event updates, direct links, local reach. |
| **LinkedIn** | 1 time per day (Mon–Fri) | 50–100 chars for links; up to 1,500 for storytelling | Professional networking, thought leadership, company culture. |
| **X (Twitter)** | 2–5 times per day | 71–100 chars (leaves room for easy quote-reposting) | Real-time updates, customer service, industry news. |
| **TikTok** | 1–3 times per day | ~100 chars (keep captions brief; let the video speak) | Short-form video, trends, educational hooks, entertainment. |

---

### 2. The 14-Day Content Blueprint

Below is a rotating two-week plan. Rather than posting on every platform every day, focus the content on the platforms that make the most sense for that specific industry.

#### 🍔 Restaurants (Focus: IG, TikTok, FB)
*Visuals and cravings drive restaurant engagement. Keep captions short and mouth-watering.*
* **Week 1**
    * **Monday:** Menu Highlight. Close-up video of a popular dish. *(IG/TikTok)*
    * **Wednesday:** Behind-the-Scenes (BTS). Quick clip of the chef preparing a meal or kitchen banter. *(IG Reels/TikTok)*
    * **Friday:** Weekend Promo. A high-energy photo of a cocktail or special to drive weekend foot traffic. *(FB/IG)*
* **Week 2**
    * **Tuesday:** Local Supplier Feature. Tag the local bakery or farm you get ingredients from. *(IG/FB)*
    * **Thursday:** Team Spotlight. A photo of a bartender or server with a quick, fun fact about them. *(IG/FB)*
    * **Saturday:** User-Generated Content. Repost a great photo a customer took (with permission). *(IG)*

#### 🔧 Home Services (Focus: FB, IG, Nextdoor)
*Trust and expertise are your selling points. Show the transformation and the people doing the work.*
* **Week 1**
    * **Monday:** Before & After. Carousel post showing a messy yard/broken pipe vs. the clean, finished result. *(FB/IG)*
    * **Wednesday:** Quick Maintenance Tip. Short video explaining how to change a filter or prep a lawn. *(IG/TikTok)*
    * **Friday:** Customer Review. A graphic featuring a 5-star review from a happy client in Omaha or Lincoln. *(FB/IG)*
* **Week 2**
    * **Tuesday:** Meet the Crew. A photo of the team standing by the work trucks. Builds safety and trust. *(FB/IG)*
    * **Thursday:** Common Myth Busted. E.g., "Do you really need to leave your AC running all day?" *(FB/IG)*
    * **Saturday:** Seasonal Prep Checklist. A simple graphic listing 3 things homeowners should do this month. *(FB/Nextdoor)*

#### 🛍️ Retail (Focus: IG, TikTok, FB, Pinterest)
*Aesthetics and urgency drive retail. Show the product in action.*
* **Week 1**
    * **Monday:** New Arrival Drop. High-quality carousel or video of new inventory. *(IG/TikTok)*
    * **Wednesday:** Styling/Usage Tutorial. How to wear a piece or style an item in a living room. *(IG Reels/TikTok)*
    * **Friday:** Flash Sale or Weekend Vibe. A short, urgent post driving traffic to the site or store. *(FB/IG)*
* **Week 2**
    * **Tuesday:** Customer Spotlight. Share a photo of a customer wearing or using your product. *(IG/FB)*
    * **Thursday:** Packaging/Shipping BTS. A satisfying, fast-paced video of packing an order. *(TikTok/IG Reels)*
    * **Saturday:** Staff Picks. A graphic or short video showing what the employees are buying right now. *(IG/FB)*

#### 💼 Professional Services (Real Estate, Legal, Consulting) (Focus: LinkedIn, FB, IG)
*Authority and relationships are the product. Educate your audience and humanize your brand.*
* **Week 1**
    * **Monday:** Market Update / Industry News. A breakdown of a current trend and what it means for the client. *(LinkedIn/FB)*
    * **Wednesday:** Case Study / Just Sold. Highlighting a recent client win and the strategy used to get it. *(LinkedIn/IG)*
    * **Friday:** Office Culture. A casual photo of the team at lunch or celebrating a milestone. *(IG/FB)*
* **Week 2**
    * **Tuesday:** FAQ Answered. A short video answering the most common question you get from new clients. *(IG Reels/LinkedIn)*
    * **Thursday:** Data/Infographic. A clean chart showing relevant stats (e.g., local market appreciation). *(LinkedIn/FB)*
    * **Friday:** Personal "Why". A text-heavy storytelling post about why you started in this industry. *(LinkedIn)*

#### 🏢 General Business (B2B, SaaS, Misc.) (Focus: LinkedIn, X, FB)
*Value and thought leadership drive B2B engagement. Focus on solving problems.*
* **Week 1**
    * **Monday:** Product/Service Deep Dive. Highlighting one specific feature that solves a major pain point. *(LinkedIn/X)*
    * **Wednesday:** Article/Blog Share. Link to a helpful piece of content with a 2-sentence summary of the main takeaway. *(LinkedIn/X)*
    * **Friday:** Company News. Announcing a new hire, a software update, or an upcoming event. *(LinkedIn/FB)*
* **Week 2**
    * **Tuesday:** How-To/Mini-Tutorial. A quick screen-recording or graphic showing how to achieve a specific result. *(LinkedIn/X)*
    * **Thursday:** Client Testimonial. A quote graphic from a business owner who saved time or money using your service. *(LinkedIn/FB)*
    * **Friday:** Friday Motivation/Leadership Tip. A short, punchy insight on management or business growth. *(LinkedIn/X)*
