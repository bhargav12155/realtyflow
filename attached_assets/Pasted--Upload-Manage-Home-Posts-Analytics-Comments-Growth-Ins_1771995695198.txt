Creating branded content on TikTok
Whether you're participating in collaborations, endorsements, or ambassadorships, partnering with brands gives you access to more rewards, more opportunities, and more viewers . And it offers brands the chance to connect more authentically with the unique community you've built. It's a win-win for everyone! Following branded content requirements When you post content that promotes a brand, product, or service (including your own brand or business) your content must: Adhere to our Terms of Service , Community Guidelines , Branded Content Policy (if applicable), and all applicable laws and regulations. Have the content disclosure toggle turned on. This ensures that you're transparent about the type of content you're posting by clearly indicating to TikTok users that it is commercial in nature. If you don't display the proper disclosure, your content may not be eligible for the For You Feed. &nbsp;&nbsp;If you're promoting your own brand or business, your post will be labeled as \" Promotional content .\" If you're posting branded content on behalf of another business, your post will be labeled as \" Paid partnership .\" The content disclosure setting is available for organic posts only. Paid ads via TikTok One are labelled automatically. Turning on the content disclosure setting From the post screen: Create a post. On the post screen, tap Content disclosure and ads. If you're not enrolled in TikTok One, tap More options ... and tap Content disclosure and ads. Turn the Disclose commercial content setting on. Select whether you're promoting your brand or branded content (such as a third-party brand, product, or service). Tap Brand partnership. On the next screen, search for the brand that you've partnered with and add it. Tap Save , then tap Continue to confirm. Exit out of the More options menu then tap Post . From your post: Go to your profile and select the post. Tap the More options ... Button on the side of the post. Tap Ad settings at the bottom. You may need to scroll across. Turn the Disclose commercial content setting on, then select whether you're promoting your own brand or creating branded content. Tap Brand partnership . On the next screen search for the brand that you've partnered with and add. Tap Save , then tap Continue to confirm. Landing a brand partnership Finding opportunities to work with brands all starts with one thing: creating authentic content . Brands want to collaborate with creators who can make sponsored posts feel natural on the For You feed. So, posting engaging, genuine videos is one of the best ways to get them to want to work with you. You can also start talking about your favorite brands in your videos to create content that brands will want to Spark . If you're looking for more ways to get yourself in front of brands, then your next step is to join TikTok One . Connect with brands, manage collaborations, and apply for open brand campaigns all in one place! The platform even gives you access to creative resources like trending content and best practices, so you can give yourself the greatest shot at landing brand partnerships. Who is eligible to participate in Creator Rewards Program?
The Creator Rewards Program is an amazing opportunity to collect big rewards for posting your original content. Want to join in the fun? Make sure you meet the requirements below! Eligibility requirements The program is currently open to creators in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, and Brazil , so you must be based in one of these countries and have an account registered there. Your TikTok account should be in good standing , meaning you follow our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service . Only Personal Accounts are eligible. Business Accounts, accounts associated with organizations, and accounts associated with political or government institutions do not qualify. You need to be at least 18 years old to participate. Your account should have at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the last 30 days. You must not tamper with the program, engage in fraudulent activities, or violate our Community Guidelines or Creator Code of Conduct in any way. Keep it real by posting original content that's authentic to you. What's an account in good standing? TikTok will periodically review accounts for eligibility in the Creator Rewards Program to ensure compliance with our monetization policies. During these reviews, we check your account information and recent videos for the following ineligible factors: Unoriginal content: Your account info or video content is copied from others or has minimal original input or edits. Low quality: Your account info or video content includes split screens, meaningless reactions, low-quality images, or slide videos. Advertising: Your profile or account info contains personal or business contact information. Clickbait: Your profile, account info, or content lacks meaningful value and is primarily designed to attract followers, likes, or advertisement clicks. Inappropriate: Your account or content contains inappropriate audio, behavior or visuals, such as sexually suggestive content and excessive body exposure. Disturbing: Your account or content has graphics or scenes that could upset or shock viewers. Security issue: Your account shows unusual or suspicious activity, such as artificial engagement, manipulation of the rewards system, harassment of other users, or involvement in illegal activities. Community guideline violations: Your account contains content that violates our Community Guidelines . Please refer to the detailed policy to avoid future violations. Content violation threshold: Your account has 5 video violations in the past 30 days. Account violation threshold: Your account has 5 total account violations, which will make you permanently ineligible for the program. Government, Politician, and Political Party Accounts: Your account is identified as a \"Government, Politician, and Political Party Account,\" which makes you ineligible for the program. See our policy for more details. Underage account: Your account does not meet the age requirements for the program, and is therefore ineligible. See our policy for more details. Business account: Your account is a Business account, making you ineligible for the Creator Rewards Program. Creator code of Conduct: Your account has been restricted due to violations of the Creator Code of Conduct, making you ineligible for the program. Learn more about our Creator Code of Conduct . If you no longer have access to the Creator Rewards Program due to violations that affect your account standing, such as your TikTok account being permanently banned, you will forfeit any outstanding rewards from the program. If you successfully appeal and your account is recovered, you will automatically be reinstated in the Creator Rewards Program. How to switch a business account to a personal account As mentioned above, you must have a personal account to participate in the Creator Rewards Program. If you currently have a business account, you can change it by going to Settings and Privacy → Account → \" Switch to personal account .\" After you switch to a personal account, you'll be able to apply for the Creator Rewards Program after 30 days. How to switch from a private account to a public account You also need to make sure your account is set to public to be eligible for the program. Here's how to make sure your account is public: Ensure you have the latest version of the TikTok app. Go to your Profile . Tap the menu \" ≡ \" in the top right corner. Select Settings and privacy . Tap Privacy . Next to \" Private account ,\" adjust the toggle to OFF. How to submit an appeal if your video becomes ineligible If you receive a notification that says your video doesn't meet the eligibility criteria, you can submit an appeal within 80 days . You can view any video disqualifications from your TikTok Inbox, as well as on your Creator Rewards Program Dashboard under your 30-day to all-time earnings. To submit an appeal for your removed video: Navigate to the video that was removed. Tap \" Community Guidelines violation: See details .\" Tap \" Submit an appeal ,\" then follow the instructions provided. How to submit an appeal if your account has been disqualified If you're notified that your account has been disqualified from the Creator Rewards Program, you can submit an appeal within 30 days . To submit an appeal from your inbox: In your TikTok app, tap Inbox . Select the notification. Tap \" Submit an appeal ,\" then follow the instructions provided. You can also submit an appeal from your Profile: Go to your Profile Tap the menu \" ≡ \" in the top right corner. Go to TikTok Studio . Tap on the Creator Rewards Program .
